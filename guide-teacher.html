Instructor Guide

How to use the instructor dashboard, step by step.

1. Log in2. Home and the bell3. Approve students4. Activate a student5. Watch a student live6. Evaluate7. Scores and printing8. More actions9. Training Modules10. Question Bank

1. Log in

  1. Open the DejaView website and type your 10-digit Employee ID (or your email) and your password.
  2. To change your password, press your name at the top right, then Change password.
  3. Forgot your password? Type your email or Employee ID on the login page, press Forgot password?, and open the email we send you (check Spam too). If your account has no email saved yet, ask the administrator.

2. Home and the bell

  • Home shows how many students are active, waiting for approval, playing now and waiting for your evaluation.
  • The bell lists what needs your attention (pending students, maps to evaluate, accounts that will expire).

3. Approve students

  1. Open Pending.
  2. Press Approve only for students you recognize. Reject deletes the registration (the student can register again).

4. Activate a student

  1. Open Student List. It starts on All my sections; choose one section to see only its students.
  2. Choose the map (or all 3 maps) next to Activate for.
  3. Press Activate on the student whose turn it is. Activate one student at a time.
  4. The student can now log in to the VR app and play. A map locks after the student finishes it. Deactivate stops the access.

5. Watch a student live

  1. Open Live. Each playing student shows a card: where they are, the question, the key points of a good answer and the clocks.
  2. To see the student's screen, type the phone's IP address under the heading (on the phone: Settings > Wi-Fi > the connected network > IP address) and press Open live view.
The student plays on their own phone (with the DejaView app installed). You can watch on any laptop, tablet or TV that is connected to the same Wi-Fi as the student's phone. If the card has a green Watch live button, press it instead.

6. Evaluate

  1. In the Student List, press Evaluate on the student.
  2. Type the score for each criterion under each map. The maximum is shown next to the criterion, and every criterion has a short explanation under its name.
  3. Write your remarks (optional, up to 200 characters).
  4. Save draft keeps it hidden from the student. Submit & release makes it the final score: the student sees it and it shows under Scores.
  5. A released map is locked. Press Edit above that map to change it, then press Submit & release again.
  6. After a map is released, the Evaluate button in the Student List becomes Scores. Open it to see the grades and remarks, print the report, or press Edit grades to change something.

7. Scores and printing

  • Scores shows all maps of the student, the remarks and the average of all maps. Press Print / Save as PDF for the report.
  • Print score sheet (PDF) in the Student List prints the whole section.
  • In the print window, choose Save as PDF.

8. More actions

  • Reset a finished map: the student can play that map again, and its saved evaluation is deleted. Then press Activate again.
  • Edit name, Renew account (extends the account) and Archive student (keeps the records, removes the student from the active list).

9. Training Modules

  1. Open Training Modules and choose the destination tab.
  2. Press + Add Module. Choose Lesson (students read it on their page) or Game history (the story told at a stop in the game).
  3. Tick the sections that can see it (only your own sections). Students in other sections never see it, and other instructors cannot see or change your modules.
  4. For a game history, choose the stop name, write the history, and add up to 4 photos with captions. A new paragraph becomes a new screen in the game.
Only the photos and text you save here are shown in the game. The stop name must match the game's stop.

10. Question Bank

  1. Open Question Bank and choose the destination. Each Question number has the original question (read only) and the questions you added.
  2. Press + Add question: choose the sections that get it (only your own sections), the speaker, the question the student answers out loud, and the key points of a good answer (only you see them).
  3. You can edit or delete only your own questions. Other instructors cannot see them, and your questions never reach their students.
  4. Each student gets one question per number, picked at random from the original and the ones written for their section. In Scores you can see which questions that student was really asked.
