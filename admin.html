Grading Criteria

Rename the criteria, add new ones, remove the ones you do not need, and set the maximum points and a short explanation for each. The maximums must add up to exactly 100. Students see the criteria and the explanations in the game before each map and on their page. This also applies to every section, the instructors' Evaluate window and the printouts.

CriterionExplanationMax points

Tip: set this before instructors start grading. Scores that were already released keep their numbers, but they will be shown against the new maximums.

Training Modules

Lessons your students read on their student page before the VR activity. Add lesson notes and an optional link to a file or video (YouTube, Google Drive, PDF). Students can only read them; they cannot edit.
