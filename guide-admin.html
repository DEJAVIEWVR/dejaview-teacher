Administrator Guide

How to manage sections, teachers and grading criteria.

1. Log in2. Home3. Sections4. Teacher accounts5. Student list6. Question Bank7. Grading criteria8. Being a teacher too

1. Log in

  1. Open the DejaView website and log in with your admin email and password.
  2. Forgot your password? Type your email on the login page and press Forgot password?. A reset link is sent to that email. For accounts made before emails were added, use the reset-password.js tool.
  3. Press your name at the top right to Edit name & title (shown on printouts), open this User Guide or Change password.

2. Home

Home shows the number of sections, teachers, active students and students waiting for approval, and one card per section (registered students, pending approvals and the teacher). The bell at the top shows what needs your attention. Open it and the red number disappears: new items are marked NEW, and the ones you have seen turn grey with Read.

3. Sections

  1. Open Sections. Type one or more section names (separated by commas) and set the capacity (students per section).
  2. Press Add Section(s). These sections appear in the VR register screen.
  3. A section that still has students cannot be deleted.

4. Teacher accounts

  1. Open Teacher Accounts.
  2. Type the full name, the 10-digit Employee ID, the teacher's own email (used for Forgot password) and a temporary password (8 or more characters, with a letter and a number).
  3. Tick every section the teacher handles and press Create Teacher.
  4. Press Sections on a teacher to change the sections. Press Archive when a teacher leaves: the teacher cannot log in, but the records stay.
  5. Above the table, change List to Archived teachers to see them and press Restore to bring one back.

5. Student list

Open Student List to see all students by section. This list is for viewing only. Instructors approve and manage their own students.

Training Modules

  1. Open Training Modules. You see every module of every teacher and can edit or delete any of them.
  2. Press + Add Module to add a lesson or a game history for all sections or only the sections you tick.
  3. A module a teacher adds reaches only the sections that teacher ticked. Students in other sections never see it.

6. Question Bank

  1. Open Question Bank and choose a map. You see everything: the original questions and every question the teachers added (with their name and sections).
  2. Only you can edit the original questions. Press + Add question to add one for all sections or only the sections you tick (up to 10 per question number). Teachers can add questions only for their own sections, and only they and you can see those.
  3. In the game, every student gets one question per number, picked at random from the original and the ones written for their section.

7. Grading criteria

  1. Open Grading Criteria.
  2. Rename a criterion, change its maximum points and write its short explanation. Press + Add criterion or the X to remove one (1 to 10 criteria).
  3. The maximums must add up to exactly 100. Then press Save criteria.
  4. Students see the criteria and explanations in the game before each map. Instructors see them in the Evaluate window.
Set the criteria before instructors start grading. Scores that were already released keep their numbers.

8. Being a teacher too

  1. Press your name at the top right and choose Also be a teacher. Type your full name and your 10-digit Employee ID, then tick the sections you will handle.
  2. After that, the same menu shows Open my teacher dashboard. On the teacher dashboard, the menu has Open admin dashboard to come back.
