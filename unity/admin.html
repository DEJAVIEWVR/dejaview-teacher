Extra Questions per Destination

What is a waypoint? A waypoint is one stop along the virtual tour where a tourist character stops the student and asks a question. Each destination (Grotto, Mt. Balagbag, Padre Pio) already has its ready-made stops. An extra stop is a spare stop that instructors can fill with their own question in the Question Bank. Use the table to choose how many extra stops each destination may have (0 to 3).
