Student Guide

How to register, play a map and see your results.

1. Register (VR app)2. Log in and pick a map3. Playing a map4. Modules and results

1. Register (VR app)

  1. Open the DejaView VR app and press Create account.
  2. Type your name, your Student ID, your section and your own email, then read the data privacy notice and agree. Your email is only used to send a password reset link. Nobody else sees it, and it is never used for anything else.
  3. Create a password (the app tells you the rules) and submit.
  4. Wait for your instructor to approve your account.

2. Log in and pick a map

  1. Log in with your Student ID and password.
  2. Forgot your password? Type your Student ID, tap Forgot password?, then open the email we send you (check Spam too) and choose a new password. If your account was made before emails were added, ask your instructor.
  3. Your instructor activates you when it is your turn. If you are not activated, the app tells you.
  4. Look at a map card and press X to travel. Finish the maps in order: Level 1 first.

3. Playing a map

  1. Look at the Tour Guide and press A to talk. He explains the rules.
  2. The Criteria board shows what your instructor will judge. Press A to read the next page.
  3. Follow the arrows on the ground to the next stop. Step into the glowing ring and read the story. Press A for the next screen.
  4. The visitors then ask you a question. Answer out loud like a real tour guide. You have 30 seconds. Press A when you are done.
  5. The map clock (5 minutes) only runs while you read stories and answer. Walking does not use time.
  6. At the end, the result board shows. Your instructor will evaluate you. Press A to go back to the maps.
Each map is a one-time test. If something went wrong with your phone, tell your instructor: they can reset the map for you.

4. Training modules and your results (VR app)

  • After you log in, the Student Dashboard has your Training Modules (lessons and stories for each destination) and your records (your scores).
  • Read the training modules before you play a map.
  • Your scores appear after your instructor releases them. They show every map, your remarks and your average.
Students use the VR app only. The website is for instructors and the administrator.
